ABOUT THE PROJECT

About app

Messianic Scripture App is a Scripture reader featuring Cebuano text and the English King James Version. The Cebuano Bible is bundled locally; KJV books are downloaded when first requested and retained in your browser cache when supported.

⌑
YOUR DATA

Privacy & policy

The app does not require an account or send reading activity to a server. KJV text is retrieved from a public-domain Bible data repository when needed. Browser storage and cache behavior depends on your browser and how this app is opened.
